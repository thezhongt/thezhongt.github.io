About Me

who I am, where I'm going

I study computer science and lead the Maryland AI Safety Initiative at the University of Maryland. I'm a researcher, builder, and thinker at the intersection of AI safety and futurism. I believe that recursive self-improvement (think expert-level R&D capabilities) is on the horizon and will revolutionalize society as we know it. I'm interested in questions about what the ideal future looks like, and how we can take action through research and governance to get there.

I'm currently taking this summer to explore myself and the world around me. I'll be traveling (spending June in Japan, studying abroad in Zurich in the fall!), answering questions that interest me, and creating content. I'm excited to express myself and my ideas through writing and videos :)
